## Object

The Andromeda Galaxy - M31 in the Messier Catalogue, and NGC 224, is approximately 2.5 million light years from earth and the farthest object visible to the naked eye.

M31 is the largest galaxy in our Local Group, a diameter of approx. 152,000 ly and has an apparent magnitude of 3.4 - one of the brightest objects in the Messier catalogue.

## Imaging Project

The FOV with this camera and scope is nice and big and gets the whole of Andromeda in - 3.74° x 2.5°.

<figure class="image"><img style="aspect-ratio:668/486;" src="/assets/images/projects/m31-andromeda/object-project-notes-image.png" width="668" height="486"></figure>

The plan is to shoot at least 5 hours in each channel, plus an additional 5 hours of Ha, and then to process the best 80% of these.

This means I need:

*   5 hours = 300 minutes
*   300 / 3 minute subs = 100 individual subs for each channel
*   100 = 80%, so actually need 125 subs to give this
*   125 \* 3 = 375 minutes
*   375 minutes / 60 mins per hour = 6.25 hours per channel
*   6.25 \* 4 = 25 hours total imaging time + the Hα 5 hours
*   A total of 400 subs to process, plus 30 flats per channel, 30 flat-darks per channel, 30 darks for the lights = 580 subs in total
